Typical Problems
Without Integration

Sound Familiar? Many RMC and batching plants still operate with little or no integration between plant and ERP.

  • Manual tracking of production, raw materials, and returns
  • Lack of real-time visibility on what is produced, loaded, and delivered
  • Material wastage and leakages due to no batch-wise consumption control
  • Delayed MIS reports and mismatches between plant reports and accounts
  • Difficulty in calculating accurate cost per cubic meter, per site, or per project

How SAZS Cloud ERP + Schwing Stetter Integration Solves This

Our industry-focused ERP integration addresses these pain points. Five targeted solutions built specifically for ready mix concrete plant operations.

04

05
Cloud-based access from anywhere
Access ERP securely from office, home, or on the go. Manage multiple plants from a single cloud platform.
