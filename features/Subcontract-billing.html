About the Module

Subcontract Billing for Your Projects

Our Subcontract Billing software helps construction and infrastructure companies manage subcontractor contracts from work order to final payment — all within a single, integrated ERP platform.

From creating BOQ-linked work orders at the project level to raising and approving Running Account (RA) bills with digital measurement books, every step is automated, auditable, and connected to your accounts and project costing modules.

Built for compliance — with GST, TDS, and retention recovery built directly into every billing cycle.

BOQ-based work orders linked to project and cost centre — with digital measurement book and quantity validation.

Running Account (RA) billing with running totals and full reconciliation reports at final settlement.

Automatic GST, TDS, retention, and advance recovery applied on every billing cycle. Automatically.

Multi-level approval from site to management — with real-time cash-flow visibility for each subcontractor.

Direct integration with the project costing module for end-to-end financial control.

Manage all your subcontractor bills at a glance. No more Excel sheets or manual entries!

Key Capabilities

Everything You Need for Subcontract Management

Purpose-built features for construction subcontracting — from site measurement to final account closure.

01

BOQ-Based Work Orders

Create subcontract work orders directly linked to your Bill of Quantities, project, and cost centre. Every rupee is traceable to a project activity.

02

Digital Measurement Book

Record site measurements digitally with quantity validation. Eliminates manual errors and ensures billing stays within sanctioned quantities.

03

RA Billing & Advances

Raise Running Account bills with auto-computed advance recovery, retention deductions, and price variation — all mapped to the work order.

04

GST, TDS & Retention Auto-Calc

All statutory deductions — GST under RCM, TDS under IT and GST acts, and retention holdbacks — calculated automatically on every bill.

05

Multi-Level Approvals

Configurable approval workflows from site engineer → project manager → accounts → management. Digital sign-off at every stage with full audit trail.

06

Integrated Project Costing

Every payment posts directly to accounts and updates project cost reports and cash-flow forecasts in real time — no double data entry.
