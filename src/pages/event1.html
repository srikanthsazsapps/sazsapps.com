Event Overview

A Landmark Industry Engagement

The CODDISSIA Stone Quarry, Crushers & Lorry Owners Association Expo 2023 represents a defining milestone in our corporate trajectory—marking our inaugural participation in a large-scale, industry-focused trade exhibition.

Hosted at the prestigious CODDISSIA Trade Fair Complex in Coimbatore, this expo convened a concentrated network of professionals from the stone quarrying, crushing, and logistics sectors across Tamil Nadu. The platform facilitated structured interactions with industry leaders, business proprietors, and operational decision-makers actively engaged in core industrial operations.

As exhibitors, we capitalized on this opportunity to demonstrate our solutions in a live business environment, engage in requirement-driven consultations, and showcase our operational capabilities to a highly targeted audience. This event proved instrumental in amplifying our market visibility and validating our offerings within this specialized industrial ecosystem.

Strategic Impact: This expo marked the beginning of our expansion into structured industry engagements and B2B exhibitions, laying a solid foundation for future participation in regional and national trade events. The insights acquired, relationships cultivated, and commercial momentum generated have contributed substantially to our long-term growth strategy.
Foundation for Growth

Building Industry Relationships

Through direct engagement and live demonstrations, we successfully established meaningful connections with quarry owners, crusher operators, and logistics professionals. The face-to-face interactions enabled us to understand real-world operational challenges and present tailored technological solutions.

The event generated strong commercial traction with measurable business outcomes. We successfully acquired 60+ new customers, doubling our customer base at that stage, and generated 300+ qualified business contacts enabling sustained post-event follow-ups and long-term client acquisition.
