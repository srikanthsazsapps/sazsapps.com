Event Overview

International Mining Expo & Industry Conclave

The International Mining Expo & Conclave (IEMS) 2024, held at the prestigious Chennai Trade Centre, emerged as a high-impact international forum bringing together mining industry leaders, technology innovators, regulatory experts, and operational stakeholders from across India and global markets.

The event focused on advancing safety, operational excellence, sustainability, and digital transformation within the mining and quarrying ecosystem. Our participation as the Technology Partner at IEMS 2024 marked a defining moment in our organizational journey.

Strategic Milestone:

This event coincided with our formal transition into a Private Limited company, signifying a strategic shift toward enhanced corporate governance, scalability, and enterprise-level engagement.
