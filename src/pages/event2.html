Event Overview

South Asia's Premier Construction Equipment Expo

EXCON 2023, organized by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), is globally recognized as South Asia's largest and most influential construction equipment and infrastructure exhibition, serving as a strategic convergence point for stakeholders across construction, mining, infrastructure development, material processing, and heavy equipment manufacturing sectors.

Our participation as an exhibitor at EXCON 2023 represented a critical phase in our organizational expansion, marking our second large-scale industry engagement and a decisive move toward strengthening our national presence. The exhibition provided a highly structured B2B environment for enterprise-level discussions, technology evaluation, and solution-driven engagement with industry leaders and operational decision-makers.

Strategic Significance:

This event marked our transition from regional player to national contender, establishing credibility through CII's internationally recognized platform and enabling engagement with enterprise-level clients across diverse geographical markets.

Product Launch

Bricks Software Unveiled at EXCON 2023

A comprehensive digital solution engineered to streamline operations, enhance process visibility, and support data-driven decision-making within construction material and infrastructure-related businesses.

Launch Ceremony

The official launch of Bricks Software was formally inaugurated by Mr. T. Premkumar of TTV Blue Metals, Sankarankovil, a key representative of the TTV Group and grandson of the founder Mr. Thirumalai Nadar. His presence added strong industry significance and legacy value to the launch.

Comprehensive Solution Architecture

The Bricks Software addresses core operational challenges such as workflow optimization, operational control, reporting accuracy, and scalability across multi-site operations. Live demonstrations and technical walkthroughs enabled detailed discussions around system architecture, operational integration, and real-world application scenarios.

  • Advanced reporting and analytics capabilities

  • Integration-ready architecture for existing systems
