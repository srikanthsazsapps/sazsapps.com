Key Capabilities

Comprehensive features designed specifically for stone crushing and quarry operations

Automated Weighbridge Operations

Capture weight data directly from the scale, eliminating manual entry and operator fraud. Real-time integration ensures 100% accuracy.

Vehicle & Trip Management

Track trip cycles, fuel consumption, and driver performance to reduce operational costs. Monitor fleet efficiency in real-time.

Inventory Control

Maintain real-time stock levels of different aggregate sizes to prevent stockouts or overproduction.
