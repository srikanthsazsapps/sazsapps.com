Event Overview

South Asia's Premier Construction Expo

EXCON 2025, organized by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), is recognized as South Asia's largest and most influential construction equipment and infrastructure exhibition, serving as a premier platform for showcasing advanced technologies, integrated solutions, and large-scale industrial innovations.

Our participation at EXCON 2025 represented the most advanced and mature phase of our product and organizational journey, reflecting years of continuous development, integration, and operational refinement. This event served as a comprehensive demonstration of our end-to-end, enterprise-ready software ecosystem built for complex industrial operations.

Organizational Maturity:

EXCON 2025 marked the culmination of years of development, showcasing a fully integrated, automation-driven platform with enterprise-scale capabilities across construction materials, mining, and infrastructure sectors.
