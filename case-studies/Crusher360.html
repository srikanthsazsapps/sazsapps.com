Managing a Crusher Plant in 360°

Crusher plant operations are fast-paced, high-volume, and highly dependent on coordination between production, dispatch, inventory, and machinery. Even small inefficiencies can lead to major losses in productivity and revenue.

Managing all this manually or through disconnected systems results in delays, stock mismatches, equipment downtime, and billing errors. Sazs ERP delivers a complete 360° solution.

The problem

Why Crusher Plants Need Sazs ERP

Crusher businesses face critical operational challenges that manual or disconnected systems simply cannot resolve. Sazs ERP unifies all these processes into one intelligent platform.

01
Inconsistent production tracking

No reliable view of raw input vs finished output across production stages.

02
Frequent machine breakdowns

Unplanned downtime with no preventive maintenance system in place.

03
Dispatch delays & mismanagement

Vehicle scheduling done manually leads to costly operational delays.

04
Manual weighbridge errors

Weight mismatches at the gate cause direct revenue leakage on every load.

05
Stock inaccuracies across sizes

M-sand, 20mm, 40mm and more tracked without live inventory data.

06
Complex billing & customer management

GST billing and credit tracking managed in fragmented, disconnected systems.

360° Management

9 Core Modules of Sazs ERP

Every aspect of crusher plant operations covered in one unified platform — from raw material input to financial reporting and compliance.

1
Production

Production Monitoring & Control

  • Track raw material input vs finished output in real time
  • Stage-wise crushing — primary, secondary, and screening
  • Output tracking by size: M-Sand, P-Sand, and aggregates
  • Machine-wise efficiency monitoring across the plant
2
Inventory

Material Stock & Yard Management

  • Live stock tracking by material type and size
  • Yard-wise inventory visibility across all locations
  • Automated stock updates from production and dispatch
  • Wastage and loss analysis to reduce material leakage
3
Weighbridge

Weighbridge Automation

  • Direct integration with weighbridge hardware systems
  • Automatic weight capture — zero manual entry needed
  • Vehicle entry/exit tracking at every gate point
  • Trip-based load management for complete billing accuracy
4
Logistics

Dispatch & Fleet Management

  • Vehicle scheduling and allocation with load planning
  • Dispatch tracking from gate exit to delivery point
  • Optional GPS integration for real-time fleet location
  • Delivery confirmation and live status updates
5
Sales & Billing

Sales, Billing & Customer Management

  • Instant GST-compliant invoice generation
  • Rate management based on customer and product type
  • Credit tracking and outstanding balance management
  • Faster collections and improved customer satisfaction
6
Maintenance

Machine Maintenance & Downtime Control

  • Preventive maintenance schedules for every machine
  • Real-time breakdown logging and instant alerts
  • Spare parts tracking and consumption analysis
  • Equipment performance analytics over time
7
Finance

Financial Management & Cost Control

  • Cost per ton calculation for every product type
  • Fuel, labor, and operational expense tracking
  • Integrated accounting and financial reporting
  • Profit & loss reporting with real-time margin visibility
8
Workforce

Workforce & Contractor Management

  • Attendance tracking and shift allocation
  • Payroll automation with minimal manual effort
  • Contractor billing and performance management
  • Boost workforce productivity across all departments
9
Analytics

Reports, Analytics & Compliance

  • Real-time dashboards for production vs sales analysis
  • Stock and dispatch reports generated on demand
  • Compliance-ready documentation at all times
  • Stay informed and audit-ready with structured data
