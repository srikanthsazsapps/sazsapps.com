About the Module

Subcontract Billing for Indian Contractors

Our Subcontract Billing software helps Indian construction and infrastructure companies manage subcontractor contracts from work order to final payment — all within a single, integrated ERP platform.

From creating BOQ-linked work orders at the project level to raising and approving Running Account (RA) bills with digital measurement books, every step is automated, auditable, and connected to your accounts and project costing modules.

Built ground-up for Indian compliance — with GST, TDS, and retention recovery built directly into every billing cycle.
  • BOQ-based work orders linked to project and cost centre
  • Digital measurement book with quantity validation
  • Running Account (RA) billing with running totals
  • Automatic GST, TDS, retention, and advance recovery
  • Multi-level approval from site to management
  • Final settlement with reconciliation reports
  • Direct integration with project costing module
  • Real-time cash-flow visibility for each subcontractor
