FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the SAZS Cloud ERP + Schwing Stetter RMC integration.

Request Demo
What exactly is integrated between Schwing Stetter PLC and SAZS ERP?

Our integration connects the Schwing Stetter batching plant PLC directly with SAZS Cloud ERP. The following functions are linked end-to-end: mix design management and control, customer order entry and pricing, dispatch planning and vehicle allocation, batch-wise production details from the PLC, and cement, aggregate, water, and admixture consumption per batch.

What problems does this integration solve for RMC plants?

The integration addresses: manual tracking of production, raw materials, and returns; lack of real-time visibility on what is produced, loaded, and delivered; material wastage and leakages due to no batch-wise consumption control; delayed MIS reports and frequent mismatches between plant reports and accounts; and difficulty in calculating accurate cost per cubic meter, per site, or per project.

Can I monitor cost per cubic meter automatically?

Yes. Production and consumption data flow directly to costing and accounts. You can monitor cost per cubic meter, per customer, and per project, and quickly identify deviations or losses — without waiting for month-end manual reports or reconciliation.

Is this available for plants in India and the Middle East?

Yes. This solution is ideal for standalone and group plants in the UAE, Middle East, and India. The system has flexible configuration for reports, tax rules, and regional requirements — making it suitable for different operational and compliance needs across these regions.

How does dispatch and billing work with the integration?

Customer orders, pricing, and delivery schedules are handled in ERP and linked to actual plant batches. Dispatch, trip sheets, and delivery challans are generated from the same system, reducing manual errors and ensuring billing matches actual delivery quantities — resulting in faster and more accurate billing with fewer disputes and adjustments.

Who designed and built this integration?

This integration is built on a robust, scalable technical framework designed by our CTO, Mr. Zeno Allwin, and implemented by the SAZS development team. It was successfully deployed at a Schwing Stetter Ready Mix Concrete Plant PLC in the United Arab Emirates.
