FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Common questions about SAZS Fleet Management answered clearly.

How does GPS fleet tracking work in SAZS Apps? +
SAZS Apps integrates with GPS devices installed in your vehicles to provide real-time location tracking. The system shows live positions on a map, records complete route history, and tracks stoppage time. You can replay any trip to see exact routes, speed, and idle time at each location.
Can SAZS Apps detect and prevent fuel theft? +
Yes. SAZS Apps integrates with fuel sensors in vehicle tanks to monitor fuel levels in real time. If there is a sudden drop in fuel level without corresponding vehicle movement, you receive instant alerts for suspected theft or leakage.
How does tyre life tracking help reduce costs? +
The system tracks each tyre from fitment to retirement. By monitoring tyre performance and getting timely rotation alerts, you can extend tyre life by 15–25%. Cost per kilometre reports help you choose better tyre brands and avoid premature failures.
Is SAZS suitable for quarry and crusher operations? +
Absolutely. SAZS is specifically designed for quarry, crusher, RMC, and construction logistics. The system handles heavy-duty vehicles like tippers, tankers, and trucks. It integrates with your existing SAZS ERP to link fleet data with billing, weighbridge, and site operations.
What maintenance alerts does the system provide? +
SAZS provides automatic reminders based on kilometres driven, engine hours, or calendar dates. You get alerts for oil changes, filter replacements, brake checks, tyre rotation, clutch servicing, and major overhauls — with vehicle details and last service date included.
Do I need internet connection for GPS tracking? +
GPS devices in vehicles work independently and store location data locally. The system syncs data when internet connectivity is available. You can view near real-time tracking in coverage areas and see complete trip history once data is uploaded.
