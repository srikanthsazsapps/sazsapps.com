Event Overview

Sazs Apps Private Limited participated as an exhibitor at the Jharkhand Mining & Construction Show 2026 (JMC 2026), held in Ranchi, Jharkhand. The exhibition served as a premier industry platform for stakeholders across the mining, quarry, and construction sectors, enabling collaboration, technology exchange, and business networking.

As one of the key technology-focused exhibitors, Sazs Apps showcased its industry-specific ERP solutions designed to address the operational, compliance, and scalability challenges of infrastructure-driven enterprises.
