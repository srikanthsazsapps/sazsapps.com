How SAZS Apps Recovers Your Profit

The "Theft & Malpractice" Recovery

The Math

SAZS Apps Impact

The Scenario
Manual invoicing takes 15 minutes per truck. With 20 trucks a day, your staff spends 5 hours just on paperwork.
The Math
SAZS Apps reduces this to 30 seconds. You save 4.5 hours per day, allowing your staff to focus on sales or site supervision instead of data entry.
SAZS Apps Impact
Massive reduction in overhead costs and 0% dependency on specialized accountants for billing.

Without Smart Credit Control, customers often exceed their limits or delay payments by 15 days because "no one checked the ledger" during dispatch.

Automated blocks ensure you only dispatch to paying customers, drastically improving your cash flow cycle.
