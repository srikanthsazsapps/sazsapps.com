Why "General" Software Fails Your Industry

Most software is designed for trading or retail, but Crusher, Quarry, and Readymix businesses operate in "Real-Time." Here is why you need a specialized ERP:

01

The "Human Element" Risk

Generic software relies on your staff to type in the correct weight. If they make a "mistake" (or commit malpractice), the software cannot stop it. SAZS Apps ERP pulls the weight directly from the load cells.

⚠ Manual entry allows fraud and errors
⚠ No verification of actual weights
⚠ Staff can manipulate records easily
High Risk
02

The Gate Leak

Standard software doesn't know if a truck has actually left the gate or if it left with 20 tons instead of 10. Our Boom Barrier integration ensures that the physical gate only opens when the digital data is 100% correct.

⚠ No physical gate control integration
⚠ Trucks can leave without proper documentation
⚠ Material theft goes undetected
Security Gap
03

Revenue Leakage

In Brick Plants or RMC units, raw material consumption must match output. Generic software doesn't track "breakage" or "slump," leading to hidden losses. We track every unit from raw material to finished product.

⚠ No reconciliation between input and output
⚠ Hidden wastage and breakage not tracked
⚠ Profit margins erode silently

FAQ

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about SAZS ERP for Quarry, Crusher, RMC & Brick operations — weighbridge integration, GST compliance, boom barriers, and more.

Does Sazs ERP work with my existing weighbridge?

Yes. Our ERP is designed to be hardware-agnostic. We can integrate with almost all standard electronic weighbridge scales to fetch weight data automatically, eliminating manual entry.

How does the Boom Barrier integration work?

The Boom Barrier is linked directly to the ERP's dispatch module. The barrier will only trigger to open once a valid weighment is recorded and a GST-compliant invoice or delivery challan is generated, ensuring no vehicle leaves your site without proper documentation.

Can I manage multiple sites (Quarry, RMC, and Brick Plant) in one login?

Absolutely. Sazs ERP is a multi-site, multi-entity platform. You can switch between different business units or view a consolidated Owner's Dashboard to see the total performance of your entire group.

What happens if the internet goes down at the Quarry site?

Our system is designed for real-time, centralized operations, so a stable internet connection is mandatory at quarry locations. This ensures that all weighments, dispatches, invoices, and approvals are recorded instantly in the central server without delay. Unlike offline systems, our live-online architecture provides complete transparency in transactions, real-time monitoring from head office, prevention of data manipulation or backdated entries, and stronger audit control and compliance. We recommend maintaining a backup internet connection (secondary ISP or 4G/5G failover) to avoid operational interruptions.

Is our data secure? Who can see my profit and loss reports?

The ERP uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). You decide exactly what your weighbridge operator, accountant, or manager can see, and sensitive financial data and P&L reports are restricted to the owner or authorized admins only.

Does the software automatically generate E-Way Bills and E-Invoices?

Yes. Through direct API integration with the GST portal, you can generate E-Invoices (with IRN and QR codes) and E-Way bills instantly upon dispatch, without manual data entry on government websites.

Can the system handle piece-rate payments for Brick Plants?

Yes. A specialized Brick Plant Labor Management module tracks daily production counts (molding, loading, unloading) and calculates wages automatically, reducing disputes and overpayments.

How does the "Credit Limit" feature work for RMC and Quarry customers?

You can set a credit limit for every customer. If a customer places an order that exceeds their limit, or has unpaid invoices beyond a certain date, the ERP will automatically block dispatch until the payment is cleared or an override is granted by the owner.

How long does it take to set up the system?

A standard implementation, including weighbridge integration, typically takes 7 to 14 days. This includes hardware setup, data migration, and training your staff.

Do you provide training for my staff?

Yes. We provide comprehensive on-site or remote training for your operators, accountants, and managers to ensure they are comfortable using the system from day one.
