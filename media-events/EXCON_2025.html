Confederation of Indian Industry (CII)

EXCON 2025, organized by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), is recognized as South Asia's largest and most influential construction equipment and infrastructure exhibition, serving as a premier platform for showcasing advanced technologies, integrated solutions, and large-scale industrial innovations across construction, mining, quarrying, and infrastructure sectors.

Our Participation Journey

Our participation at EXCON 2025 represented the most advanced and mature phase of our product and organizational journey, reflecting years of continuous development, integration, and operational refinement. This event served as a comprehensive demonstration of our end-to-end, enterprise-ready software ecosystem built for complex industrial operations.
