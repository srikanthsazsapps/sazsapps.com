Jharkhand Mining & Construction Show 2026

Premier industry platform showcasing enterprise ERP solutions for mining, quarry, and construction sectors

Explore Our Journey

Event Overview

Sazs Apps Private Limited participated as an exhibitor at the Jharkhand Mining & Construction Show 2026 (JMC 2026), held in Ranchi, Jharkhand. The exhibition served as a premier industry platform for stakeholders across the mining, quarry, and construction sectors, enabling collaboration, technology exchange, and business networking.

As one of the key technology-focused exhibitors, Sazs Apps showcased its industry-specific ERP solutions designed to address the operational, compliance, and scalability challenges of infrastructure-driven enterprises.

Premier Technology Exhibitor

Event Timeline & Highlights

DAY 1
February 2026

Industry Engagement & Networking

The opening day focused on establishing industry connections and understanding operational challenges across mining and construction businesses. Our team engaged with plant owners, operations heads, and decision-makers, discussing current pain points related to manual processes, fragmented data, and compliance management.

Key focus areas included:

  • Digital readiness of mining and quarry operations
  • Challenges in multi-site operational control
  • Need for centralized reporting systems
DAY 2
February 2026

Digital Transformation & ERP Demonstrations

Day two centered on enterprise-level discussions around digital transformation. Sazs Apps demonstrated how its integrated ERP platform enables:

  • Real-time operational visibility across geographically distributed sites
  • Asset traceability and lifecycle management
  • Compliance alignment and statutory reporting
  • Data-driven decision-making through centralized dashboards

These interactions reinforced a clear industry shift toward centralized operations, real-time analytics, and scalable digital infrastructure to support sustainable growth.

DAY 3
February 2026

Recognition & Strategic Alignment

The final day marked the successful conclusion of the event with exhibitor recognition for Sazs Apps Private Limited. The recognition acknowledged our contribution to advancing technology-led execution in the mining and construction ecosystem.

Discussions on Day 3 focused on:

  • Long-term digital roadmaps
  • ERP implementation strategies
  • Governance-driven technology adoption

Key Industry Insights

Insights gathered across the three-day event highlighted:

ERP Demand Surge

Increasing demand for ERP software for mining and quarry operations

Transparency Focus

Growing focus on operational transparency and compliance readiness

Industry 4.0 Adoption

Adoption of Industry 4.0 principles in construction and infrastructure projects

Scalable Solutions

Need for scalable platforms supporting business expansion and governance

These trends reaffirm the importance of purpose-built ERP solutions tailored for the infrastructure sector.

🏆

Recognition & Acknowledgement

Sazs Apps Private Limited was honored as an exhibitor at Jharkhand Mining & Construction Show 2026. This recognition reflects our ongoing commitment to delivering enterprise-grade ERP solutions that enable operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and digital maturity.

FUTURE FORWARD

Looking Ahead

Participation at JMC 2026 strengthened existing partnerships and opened avenues for new collaborations across the mining and construction industry. Sazs Apps remains committed to supporting organizations in their digital transformation journey through reliable, scalable, and industry-focused ERP platforms.
